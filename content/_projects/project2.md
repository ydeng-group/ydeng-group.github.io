<h4>Overview</h4>

We combine data-driven learning with physiological knowledge to develop personalized methods for glucose prediction and control in diabetes. Our research connects wearable device data with scientific machine learning to support reliable glucose forecasts and adaptive insulin delivery strategies.

<h4>Subproject 1: Personalized Glucose Prediction</h4>

We develop **transformers**, **state space models**, and **physics-informed learning** methods to predict blood glucose from continuous glucose monitoring, insulin delivery, meals, and physical activity data. Our goal is to capture individual glucose dynamics and provide reliable forecasts that support personalized diabetes management.

<h4>Subproject 2: Adaptive Insulin Delivery and Glucose Control</h4>

We combine **physiological modeling**, **reinforcement learning**, and **model predictive control** to investigate personalized insulin delivery strategies. Our goal is to adapt dosing to changing glucose dynamics while reducing hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia.
