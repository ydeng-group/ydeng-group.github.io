<h4>Overview</h4>

We combine machine learning with physical principles and engineering knowledge to support manufacturing and autonomous vehicles. Our research focuses on translating visual information into manufacturing plans and developing autonomous vehicle control methods.

<h4>Subproject 1: Image-to-Manufacturing Process Planning for Automotive Parts</h4>

We use **language models** and **AI agents** to translate images of automotive parts into proposed manufacturing processes. Our goal is to connect visual information about part geometry and features with manufacturing knowledge, helping identify suitable operations and organize them into process plans.

<h4>Subproject 2: Autonomous Vehicle Control</h4>

We develop **machine learning** and **physics-informed control** methods for autonomous vehicles. Our goal is to combine observations of vehicle motion and the surrounding environment with physical models to guide control decisions and adapt to changing driving conditions.
